About ZIBSIP

Our History

Established in 1993 in Kitwe to provide training in business courses and other related programs. The institution started with only two programs and has grown to offer more than 15 business courses and 7 education courses.

ZIBSIP currently has approximately 78 members of staff and intends to start offering Degree programs in Commercial Studies and Business Administration shortly.
